About Goho

A practical farming companion for everyday decisions.

Goho, a Shona word meaning harvest, was created to reduce the information gap faced by farmers in Zimbabwe. The platform focuses on simple, useful farming knowledge that people can read and apply when planning crops, checking weather, learning about markets, and improving production.

Crop Doctor

Useful crop information farmers can read.

Crop Doctor currently works as an information resource. It gives farmers access to basic crop production and crop health content that can support better planting and crop care decisions.

  • Planting guidance for selected crops
  • Common crop problems and prevention tips
  • Pest and disease awareness information
  • Good farming practices for better production
